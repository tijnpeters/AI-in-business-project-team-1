# How these pages are made

Each page goes through the same steps. Only the first and the last step need a
person; every automated step also has a manual fallback.

1. **Research question** — a researcher picks one question an SME owner would ask.
2. **Collect sources** — an AI agent searches for relevant articles, reports, laws and videos.
3. **Evaluate sources** — every source is scored on the AAOCC criteria (Authority,
   Accuracy, Objectivity, Currency, Coverage). Weak sources are dropped.
4. **Translate** — Dutch sources are translated to English; the Dutch original is kept.
5. **Analyse and draft** — the agent writes a draft page with the required sections.
6. **Quality check** — the draft is checked against the project's criteria
   (traceable claims, one clear next step, plain language, legal claims flagged).
7. **Human review** — a researcher reads the draft and approves, reworks or rejects it.
8. **Publish** — only approved pages appear on this website.

## What the confidence levels mean

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **HIGH** | Based on primary or official sources (e.g. the legal text itself, an organisation's own announcement) or several independent reliable sources. |
| **MEDIUM** | Based on reliable analysis, but the situation is changing or the source has a particular perspective. Re-check before using in decisions. |
| **LOW** | Limited or single-source evidence. Treat as a signal, not a fact. |

## What we never do

- Publish a page without a researcher's approval
- Invent sources, figures, quotes or rules
- Put confidential company or interview information on this site
- Present a legal claim as settled unless it was checked against the official legal text
