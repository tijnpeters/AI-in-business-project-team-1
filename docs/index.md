# AI Handbook for Dutch technical SMEs

Short, practical pages for owners and managers of Dutch technical SMEs — companies
that design, assemble, sell or service industrial equipment. Each page answers one
question about using AI, in plain language, and ends with **one concrete thing you
can do next**.

[Browse the handbook pages](handbook/index.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[How these pages are made](about.md){ .md-button }

## What you'll find on every page

- **The question** the page answers, and why it matters for your business
- **Key findings** and **risks**, with every important claim linked to a real source
- **A checklist** you can use before you buy or start anything
- **One next step** — start a pilot, ask your IT supplier a question, compare vendors, or decide not to start yet
- **Sources with a confidence level** (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW), so you can judge how firm each claim is

!!! note "Student project"
    This handbook is made by students of the HAN Minor DDDM (*AI in Business*), with
    Waterkracht B.V. as the main case company. Pages are researched with the help of
    AI agents, but **every page is checked and approved by a human researcher before
    it appears here**. It is not legal advice: legal claims are marked as checked or
    unchecked against the official legal text.
